About Alisa Movies

Dedicated to Film Enthusiasts Worldwide

Alisa Movies is a modern, accessible web application designed to bring the magic of world cinema directly to movie lovers everywhere. Hosted seamlessly via GitHub Pages, our platform is lightweight, responsive, and completely free to explore.

Our Mission

Our mission is to celebrate cinematic storytelling across cultures and genres. Whether you are passionate about Hollywood blockbusters, Bollywood classics, Korean Dramas, or Japanese Anime, Alisa Movies provides a fast, uncluttered directory to explore synopses, release timelines, cast credits, and official trailers.

🌐 Worldwide Database

Powered by community-driven APIs including The Movie Database (TMDB) and TVMaze for accurate, up-to-date global media information.

🎥 Open Cinema Archives

Direct access to curated public domain masterpieces and Creative Commons open films from the Blender Foundation and Internet Archive.

⚡ Privacy First

Zero mandatory sign-ups. Your watchlist and preferences remain stored safely and privately in your own browser's LocalStorage.

✨ Modern Experience

Built with modern vanilla web standards, dynamic theme accents, smooth carousels, and responsive layouts across all devices.

Open Source & Free Information

We believe in an open, decentralized web. By integrating open data APIs and public domain video preservation projects, we ensure that educational film study, classic storytelling, and independent creative commons cinema remain accessible to students, creators, and enthusiasts worldwide.
