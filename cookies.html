What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide analytical reporting. In addition to cookies, modern web applications utilize HTML5 LocalStorage, which stores data locally in your browser without transmitting it across networks on every page request.

Alisa Movies strictly implements Google Consent Mode v2. By default, marketing and analytics tags remain in a restricted, denied state until you explicitly provide your affirmative consent via our GDPR banner. When consent is denied, no personal cookies or tracking identifiers are set or transmitted.

Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can configure your browser to reject all cookies, notify you when a cookie is placed, or delete existing cookies:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Third-party cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
