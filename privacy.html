Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

At Alisa Movies (accessible via our GitHub Pages website), the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received and collected by Alisa Movies and how it is used.

1. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

2. Information We Collect

Alisa Movies is built as a client-side catalog platform. We do not require users to create an account or provide personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, home address, or credit card information to browse movies, series, or public domain titles.

We may store your local viewing preferences (such as your Watchlist, Watch History, and chosen Theme Accent) directly in your browser's local storage (LocalStorage). This data resides entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our private servers.

3. Log Files

Like many other websites, Alisa Movies utilizes standard log files provided by our web hosting provider (GitHub Pages). The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This information is used solely to analyze trends, administer the site, track user movement around the site, and gather broad demographic information. IP addresses and other such information are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Browser Storage and Cookies

Alisa Movies uses browser local storage (LocalStorage) to remember your viewing preferences, selected theme accent, streaming server choices, and your personal watchlist. This information stays directly in your browser and is never transmitted to or stored on private servers.

5. Google AdSense & Advertising Partner Disclosures

Google, as a third-party advertising vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on Alisa Movies. Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve relevant advertisements to our visitors based on their visit to our website and other websites across the Internet.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that may appear on Alisa Movies. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used by our third-party advertising networks to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and to personalize the advertising content that you see. Alisa Movies has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings to personalize or opt out of Google AdSense targeted ads.
  • AboutAds Choices (DAA) to opt out of third-party advertising cookies across participating networks.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out for centralized ad privacy management.

6. Streaming Embeds and External Services

To deliver high-speed playback, our player interfaces with third-party streaming mirrors and open community metadata providers (such as TMDB and TVMaze). When video embeds load, those external services may process basic technical network data (such as IP addresses and browser agent strings) necessary to serve the video stream.

7. Third-Party Links & Security

Alisa Movies implements client-side popup shields to protect your browsing experience from unsolicited redirects or popup scripts that third-party streaming mirrors may attempt to trigger. We strongly advise users to maintain active browser security tools.

8. GDPR Privacy Rights (European Economic Area & UK)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users residing in the European Economic Area have the right to:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to erasure – You have the right to request erasure of your personal data.
  • The right to object to processing – You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.

9. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, or request the deletion of such data. We do not sell user personal data to third parties.

10. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA Compliance)

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. Alisa Movies does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

11. Contacting Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy or AdSense disclosures, do not hesitate to contact us at: alisatechstudio@gmail.com.
